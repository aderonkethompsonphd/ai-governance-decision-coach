# Internal meeting-note summarisation scenario

An organisation proposes to use an AI tool to summarise internal, non-confidential meeting notes. The summaries will not make decisions, rank people, recommend outcomes or trigger actions. Employees must review every summary before it is used, circulated or stored as an organisational record. Personal, customer, financial and health information is prohibited.

No information is supplied about the vendor, hosting, retention, model-training use, subprocessors, security testing, accuracy testing, access controls or offboarding arrangements.

## Your assessment

Assess purpose and necessity, affected people, data, autonomy and oversight, possible harms, governance and regulatory exposure, third-party dependency and reversibility. Then select an initial decision and explain the rationale.

Do not open the completed assessment until you have made your own decision.
