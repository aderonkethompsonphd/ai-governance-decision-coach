# AI applicant screening scenario

Northstar Services is a fictional company operating across several EU countries and receiving approximately 25,000 applications annually. It proposes a three-month pilot of a vendor system that analyses CVs and application responses, ranks applicants, automatically excludes applicants below a configurable score and generates short score explanations.

Recruiters would normally examine only the highest-ranked applicants. Rejected applications would generally be reviewed only after a complaint. The vendor states that the system is bias tested, 92 percent accurate, GDPR compliant and subject to human oversight, but supporting evidence has not been supplied.

## Your assessment

Assess purpose and necessity, affected people, data, autonomy and oversight, possible harms, governance and regulatory exposure, third-party dependency and reversibility. Then select an initial decision and explain the rationale.

Do not open the completed assessment until you have made your own decision.
