# Contributing

Contributions that improve the decision framework, add carefully designed fictional scenarios or strengthen accessibility are welcome.

## Scenario requirements

A contributed scenario should:

- state a clear organisational purpose and decision;
- preserve meaningful uncertainty;
- include affected-person and third-party perspectives;
- avoid presenting vendor claims as verified evidence;
- permit a proportionate range of decisions;
- distinguish inherent risk, controls, operating evidence, residual risk and uncertainty; and
- identify appropriate escalation and decision ownership.

Do not include confidential, personal or proprietary organisational information. Do not represent a fictional assessment as legal advice, certification or organisational approval.

## Suggested contribution workflow

1. Open an issue describing the proposed scenario or improvement.
2. Add or update documentation in a focused branch.
3. Explain the evidence labels, risk reasoning and expected learning outcome.
4. Submit a pull request for review.
